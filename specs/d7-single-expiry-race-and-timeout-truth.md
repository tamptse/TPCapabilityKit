## Problem Statement

A Tasks reviewer asking "where does expiry get decided?" still gets two answers in the expiry detail: the capability-wait adapter unwraps the raced outcome (timeout vs won) plus a payload cast at the registry seam, while the settlement entry unwraps the same outer outcome again to apply the stored-nil policy. The single TaskGroup topology is already shared structurally, but the double-optional decode is split across two sites — so a future caller can unwrap-and-forget the won gate, and the won gate plus the void-completion policy never read as one table. Beside it, the Bridge timeout question reverted undecided: the collapse attempt storing a resolved enum per descriptor failed against the single-store mirror pin and was reverted, leaving the dual-store-vs-single-optional choice plus the already-satisfied build-path and defaults-core follow-ups without a recorded verdict. Downstream sequencing (the constructor-contract work and the unified-decode-input work) cannot start until both halves are decided.

## Solution

Finish the single-expiry unification and record the ObjC single-truth verdict with zero behavior change: the value-carrying race becomes the one topology with the wait adapter as a pure thin map over it (no independent unwrap), and the single decode table lives adjacent to the settlement step so the won gate and the void-completion policy review as one path. On the Bridge side, close the collapse line as superseded: the single stored optional plus direct derivations already deliver policy-once plus store-once plus readers-thin, so the stored-enum alternative is rejected and the mirror pin stands unchanged. Timeout resolution (task value vs configured default, wire-negative means unspecified, omitted pins the compat literal as explicit, pin never follows reconfigure) and the single race as the only expiry seam are identical before and after.

## Implementation Decisions

- **Module**: Tasks remains the deep module behind the schedule/cancel/pending/active seam. The expiry detail keeps owning timeout-once resolution plus the single race; the time module keeps owning live plus virtual adapters and the advance policy behind one sleep seam; the mapper detail keeps owning the whole ObjC timeout fork behind its interface. No new module is introduced.
- **Interface**: the value-carrying form is canonical (timeout as empty outer vs won as present outer where the inner value may itself be absent); the `Bool` wait form is a pure thin map over it (timeout maps to `false`) applied where the flow adapts to the registry-owned race value, owning no unwrap of its own. The named race, its construction from task plus configured default, and its readable resolved timeout are frozen untouched so the downstream constructor-contract work inherits a stable shape. Decision shape: `SharedRace(task:default:clock:)` kept stable.
- **Depth**: depth decreases by one duplicated decode with zero new abstraction. The single private TaskGroup shape (one operation task plus one sleep task, first-wins, cancel-all, loser discarded never stored) is stated once; both public forms share it and only the carried payload mapping differs.
- **Seam**: the highest seams stay the Tasks schedule/cancel/pending/active facade plus the single result rendezvous and the registry whole-set wait (which keeps its `Bool`-race value parameter). Tests cross those seams; no new seam is created. The registry never names the expiry type — the flow adapts the `Bool` mapping into the existing closure value exactly as today.
- **Adapter**: no adapter change. The Bridge live Lease view, ObjC timeout mapping (wire-negative vs explicit plus pinned-compat literal), and time live/virtual adapters plus advance policy are untouched; deterministic-time spelling stays on the facade.
- **Locality**: the double-optional decode collapses to the settlement site. The flow passes the raced outcome into the settlement entry without unwrapping; the won gate (timeout path never consults the carried value) and the void-completion policy (absent result with budget retries; present-vs-absent routing when budget is exhausted) read as one truth table adjacent to the single decide-then-transition block. A bare-optional fold without the won gate is rejected, and budget/policy ownership stays in Settlement, never in the flow or the Lease.
- **Expiry-wins**: first-wins plus cancel-all stays the topology; expiry-wins means the timeout outcome dominates once taken — the late loser value is discarded by cancellation and, even if it arrives, never consults into an already-expired Lease because the won gate plus single terminal transition refuse it. No tie-break priority is added; ties remain first-completed-wins, which the deterministic suites already pin by behavior.
- **ObjC single-truth verdict (self-decided, closes the reverted line)**: the collapse line storing a resolved enum per descriptor is closed as superseded by the single stored optional plus direct derivations. Both `@objc` observables are pure derivations of the one stored optional (the enum is constructed transiently per read, never stored), so there is no second store and no sync obligation to honor — the drift hazard the stored-enum line addressed is already structurally unrepresentable. Storing the enum would reintroduce the dual-store the single-form decision deleted, and renaming the field to dodge the mirror pin would pass the check while keeping the drift the pin rejects. The mirror pin stands unchanged; no ADR amendment is proposed and the pinned-compat promise (omitted arrives as the pin literal collapsing to explicit; wire-negative stays unspecified resolved once at construction) is not reopened. The build-path and defaults-core follow-ups are recorded as already satisfied on the current head (shared descriptor-building core; single private construction core behind frozen `@objc` signatures) and need no further work.
- **Ordering**: this work lands after the value-migration and box-naming post-state and before any future address change. It assumes the terminal-unify and slot-eviction-unify post-state and must not re-split what they fused. The constructor-contract follow-up and the unified-decode-input follow-up are blocked by this work, not part of it.
- **Respects ADRs**: one-waiter-with-injectable-clock constrains the change to keep one resolved timeout feeding both paths deterministically; expiry-ownership (resolve-once plus single race for wait and execution) is fulfilled rather than reopened — this unification is the missing contract step that makes "single race" literally true; single-expiry-clock constrains the work to keep the one sleep seam and idempotent release discipline with no second clock and no second holder; the omitted-pins-compat-literal fork statement stays authoritative and untouched.

## Testing Decisions

- A good test pins externally observable behavior through the public Tasks seam with deterministic time (expired vs completed vs retry, delivered value identity, waiter counts, park-expires-once, mid-wait flip wins, late-loser discarded, already-satisfied fast path, omitted-vs-negative-vs-explicit timeout triple), never the topology spelling or holder internals — except mechanical greps proving one TaskGroup site in the expiry detail and one outer-optional decode site at settlement, plus the standing single-store mirror pin which stays green unchanged.
- Modules under test: race parity (`Bool` mapping: operation-win `true` vs timeout `false` vs operation-`false`; value form: won-with-value vs won-with-stored-nil vs timeout-empty), executor path (race-carried value into Settlement, timeout delivering expired with empty result, stored-nil retry vs fail vs expire truth table), registry wait (empty-set true, already-satisfied true, whole-set win vs single-expiry timeout, per-Capability-before-snapshot order untouched), resolve-once construction (absent resolves to default, explicit travels unchanged, ObjC wire-negative resolves to default, omitted pins compat literal), descriptor observables (both constructors, live-view wrap, client-id identity, display-plus-flag agreement with the stored optional).
- Prior art: resolve-once construction suite, execution-expiry delivery, operation-wins race, single-expiry waiter-plus-executor agreement, timeout-contract shared-expiry across mid-wait flip, value-carries-value proofs (value win, stored-nil retry/recovery, stored-nil without budget, timeout-empty, late-loser discard, parked-once, flip win, fast path), registry wait suites, Settlement and Settlement-guards (void-completion retry policy), activation-gate and slot-hold suites, ObjC mapper plus timeout-pins plus single-view suites. The unification must keep every one green with zero test-semantics edits.
- Per-ticket verification runs `swift build` plus the full `swift test` green; the mirror pin stays asserted (its suite must pass unmodified, proving the stored-enum alternative was not re-landed).

## Out of Scope

- Deleting, privatizing, or renaming the named race type; any change to timeout resolution (task value vs configured default), the pinned-compat timeout fork or literal, or the sleep seam and advance policy.
- Any change to the Settlement decision table, retry budget, void policy outcomes, waiter preservation, exactly-once delivery, or slot-release ownership (stays with activation scope-exit).
- Any change to lifecycle reachability, priority ordering, park double-park or waiter-cancel rules, generation sharing, or State creation/observation.
- Changing the registry wait interface shape, emission order, or lock discipline; generalizing the registry seam beyond `Bool`.
- Any `@objc` signature rename, removal, or deprecation; any Swift-side domain descriptor shape change; any ADR amendment.
- Splitting the Store facade, introducing a scheduler protocol, adding a lock domain, or any formatting churn beyond the race unification plus decode collapse.

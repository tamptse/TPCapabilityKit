## Problem Statement

Readiness is answered in four places with three point-in-time checks hiding one TOCTOU window: the registry offers single-capability query, per-capability Bool observation, whole-snapshot observation, and whole-set wait with an injected expiry race; the Store facade re-exposes all four as thin delegations with no conceptual narrowing; and the Tasks path checks point-in-time availability at dequeue entry, again at activation entry, and a third time post-admission inside the gate, with the entry-vs-recheck window across the scoped-hold suspension stated only as a scattered comment. The whole-set wait seam reads shallower than its signature suggests — one race plus re-query per snapshot emission — while its signature carries the full injected-race shape, so reviewers must hold registry plus Store plus Tasks together to answer "who owns set-matching and which check decides". The entry checks look decisive when they are review-only; the post-admission re-check is the decider but lives apart from the entries it corrects.

## Solution

Keep the registry as the single readiness owner and narrow by locality plus documentation, not by seam cut. The registry keeps all four answers with their existing grains: the UI pair (single-capability query plus per-capability Bool observation) for UI-style subscribers, and the Tasks-only waiter (whole-snapshot observation plus whole-set wait with one injected expiry race) for the scheduler. The Store facade keeps its thin delegations but documents them as exactly those two grains, so no caller rebuilds the wait out of snapshot observation plus re-query. The Tasks triple point-in-time check collapses conceptually into one documented availability gate beside activation: the two entries stay as early exits but point at the gate as the decider, and the entry-vs-recheck window across the hold suspension is stated once where the gate lives. Helpers stay; no method is deleted, no new seam is introduced.

## Implementation Decisions

- **Module**: the registry remains the deep module for readiness behind the whole-set wait interface; Tasks remains the deep module behind the schedule/cancel/pending/active seam. No new module is introduced; the change is prose plus gate-locality inside the existing two modules.
- **Interface**: no public seam changes. Single-capability query plus per-capability Bool observation stay the UI pair; whole-snapshot observation plus whole-set wait stay the Tasks-only waiter. The Store facade keeps all four thin delegations and groups them into those two grains in documentation only.
- **Depth**: depth is unchanged in seam count; review depth decreases by locality. The availability gate is stated once beside activation with the entry-vs-recheck window documented in one place; the two entry early-exits remain as exits but reference the gate as the decider rather than restating the window.
- **Seam**: the highest seams stay the Store query/observe facade plus the single result rendezvous and the registry whole-set wait with one injected expiry race per set. Tests cross those seams; no new seam is created and no existing seam is deleted — this is explicitly not a seam cut.
- **Adapter**: no adapter change. The Bridge live Lease view, ObjC timeout mapping, and time live/virtual adapters plus advance policy are untouched; deterministic-time spelling stays on the facade.
- **Locality**: the gate decision (terminal refusal, availability re-check, row activation) stays adjacent to activation with settling kept in the caller, so decide-then-apply reviews as one path. The hold-suspension window is stated once at the gate: entry checks are review-only early exits, the post-admission re-check decides, and admission refusal returns silently without settling.
- **Leverage**: leverage is drift removal plus window clarity: one place stating who owns set-matching, one place stating which check decides across the hold. Risk is bounded because the move is comment plus gate-locality only — same queries, same emissions, same park/expiry/activation/settlement order.
- **Ordering**: lands after the race-carries-value generalization. Assumes the value-carrying race post-state (waiter keeps the Bool specialization with identical semantics, executor carries its result through the race, registry receives the Bool race as an opaque value without naming the expiry owner); must not re-split what that work fused and must not widen the registry seam beyond Bool.
- **Respects ADRs**: registry lock with ordered emission constrains per-Capability-before-snapshot delivery to stay untouched with no caller holding the Store lock across a registry call; registry whole-set readiness forbids rebuilding the wait in callers and forbids moving park, expiry, activation, or settlement into the registry — set-matching stays in the registry, everything else stays in Tasks.

## Testing Decisions

- A good test pins externally observable behavior through the public query/observe/wait seam with deterministic time (empty-set true, already-satisfied true, whole-set win vs single-expiry timeout, emission order, entry-vs-recheck routing), never the spelling of the gate or the grouping prose — except mechanical checks that the gate comment states the window once and no caller-side wait loop appears.
- Modules under test: registry wait (empty-set skip, already-satisfied skip, missing-set expiry, partial-set expiry, arriving-set resolve through a pass-through race), emission order (per-Capability before snapshot on register and on unregister), fire agreement (missing-capability nil agreement, available-capability value agreement, waiter-convenience parity, slot-pressure sync-fire-runs-while-scheduled-wait-parks).
- Prior art: registry wait suites, emission-order suites, fire-agreement suites, activation-gate suites, slot-hold suites, single-expiry waiter-plus-executor agreement, timeout-contract shared-expiry across mid-wait flip, Settlement and Settlement-guards (void-completion retry policy). The narrowing must keep every one green with zero test-semantics edits except the new gate-locality pins.
- Per-ticket verification runs the focused suites first, then the full suite: `swift test --filter CapabilityRegistryWait`, `swift test --filter RegistryEmissionOrder`, `swift test --filter FireAgreement`, `swift test --filter ActivationGate`, ending with `swift test` for the whole package.

## Out of Scope

- Deleting or privatizing any query/observe/wait delegation, introducing a scheduler protocol, or cutting a new readiness seam.
- Any change to timeout resolution (task value vs configured default), the race topology (one operation plus one sleep, first-wins, cancelAll), or the stored-nil vs never-stored distinction.
- Any change to the Settlement decision table, retry budget, void policy, or slot-release ownership (stays with activation scope-exit).
- Any change to lifecycle reachability, priority ordering, park double-park or waiter-cancel rules, generation sharing, or State creation/observation.
- Generalizing the registry seam beyond Bool, reordering emissions, or changing lock discipline.
- Any formatting churn beyond the gate-locality plus facade-grouping prose.
